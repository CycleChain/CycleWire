# Code of Conduct

CycleWire follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone
representing the project elsewhere.

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people,
and keep technical disagreements technical. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing
someone's private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **fatih.dogancan@cyclechain.io**. Reports are read by the
maintainers of CycleChain and handled confidentially. Maintainers act on reports as the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe, from a private warning up to a
permanent ban.
